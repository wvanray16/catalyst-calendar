How to read this — and where it can be wrong

Confirmed vs. estimated is the most important field on this page. Only a handful of earnings dates in this window are actually company-announced: NVDA 8/26, MRVL 8/27, IREN 8/27, and CRDO 9/1. Everything else marked Est is derived from each company's own historical reporting pattern or a vendor consensus calendar. Most firms confirm 2–4 weeks ahead via a "sets conference call" press release — that release is itself a trackable signal, and it's when you should re-check this calendar.

Two identity findings worth flagging. SPCX is not pure SpaceX — xAI was merged in pre-IPO and the Anysphere/Cursor acquisition closed 8/14 for roughly $60B in stock, so Grok model releases and ~420M newly issued shares belong in the catalyst set alongside launch milestones. And DRAM is the Roundhill Memory ETF, not an operating company: it has no earnings of its own, so its rows here are the earnings dates of its top holdings, weighted by exposure — Micron ~25.6%, Samsung ~25.6%, SK hynix ~20.9% on a swap-plus-direct basis.

Three corrections to assumptions that would otherwise sit in this calendar. IREN discontinued its monthly operational updates after the August 2025 edition — there is no monthly drumbeat to trade in 2026. There is no NVIDIA GTC Washington DC 2026; GTC Berlin on 10/21 is the only NVIDIA-owned keynote before year-end. And the Russell semi-annual reconstitution moved from November to December (11 Dec close / 14 Dec open) — any calendar showing a November 2026 Russell recon is wrong.

The weakest-sourced items are the SPCX lockup tranches. The 8/6 first release and the ~911.5M restricted-share pool are well corroborated. The intermediate ~7% tranches, the 28% post-Q3 tranche, and whether the final expiry is 12/8 or 12/9 come only from IPO-research aggregators — the primary 424B4 was too large to retrieve. Verify against the prospectus "Shares Eligible for Future Sale" section before trading them.

Options-implied expected moves are not in this build. The FlashAlpha connector on this account is on the free tier, which gates the expected-move and earnings-analytics endpoints and caps usage at five calls a day. Where it matters, company guidance going into each print is written into the event description instead — that's the fundamental analogue of the same question.

Informational only — not investment advice. Dates change; verify anything you intend to trade against the company's own IR page.
